Activities

Some activities I enjoy are chess and volleyball. Chess has strengthened my patience and ability to think several steps ahead, while volleyball has taught me the importance of communication and relying on teammates. Both activities have reinforced skills that are valuable in software engineering, where solving a problem often requires both individual reasoning and collaboration. I also enjoy giving back to my university community. One of my most meaningful experiences has been starting a freshman leadership organization at Texas A&M. The organization is designed to help incoming students build friendships, develop their professional networks, and become more connected to campus during their first year. Through this organization, I have also participated in service initiatives, including The Big Event and philanthropic efforts supporting AVM Alliance. These experiences have shown me that leadership is not simply about holding a position. It is about creating opportunities for other people and being willing to take responsibility when something needs to be done. Overall, my experiences at Texas A&M have allowed me to grow as both a computer scientist and a person. My technical projects have challenged me to learn new technologies and solve unfamiliar problems, while my leadership and extracurricular activities have strengthened my communication, teamwork, and ability to take initiative. As I continue studying computer science, I hope to build on these experiences through professional opportunities where I can contribute to meaningful software projects, learn from other engineers, and continue developing into a well-rounded software engineer.

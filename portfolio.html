My Projects

A collection of my work and personal projects

    ← Back to Home

    My technical portfolio represents several different areas of computer science, and working across different areas has allowed me to approach software development from different perspectives and discover the types of problems I enjoy solving. One project I am particularly proud of is Aggie Agenda, a student-focused planning platform designed to help Texas A&M students keep track of important academic and campus events. I helped lead the development of the project while working with a team of students through Aggie Coding Club. The application uses React, Django, and PostgreSQL to provide students with a centralized way to organize information about their university experience. Working on Aggie Agenda gave me experience beyond simply implementing individual features. I had to think about how different components of a larger application fit together, how users would interact with the system, and how a team could coordinate development effectively. The project ultimately grew to serve hundreds of users and gave me valuable experience building software intended for a real community. Another project that reflects my interest in emerging technology is RTC, a real-time speaking coach that I developed using Rust and Tauri. The application analyzes a user's speech while they are speaking and provides information such as words per minute, filler words, pauses, and other speaking patterns. I was especially interested in this project because it combined several different technical challenges, including audio processing, real-time data collection, desktop application development, and artificial intelligence. Building RTC pushed me to work with technologies that were unfamiliar to me and strengthened my ability to learn independently when documentation or existing examples were limited. I have also explored artificial intelligence through FairPlay AI, a computer vision project designed to analyze pickleball games. The project uses computer vision techniques to track and analyze activity during a match. This project introduced me to the challenges of working with visual data and showed me how artificial intelligence can be applied to problems outside of traditional web applications. Together, these projects demonstrate my willingness to explore different areas of computer science and apply what I learn to practical problems.
